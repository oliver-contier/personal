Oliver Contier


I study how the human brain represents the world, combining large-scale brain responses with human behavior and AI models.

My work focuses on visual cognition and object representations: how does the brain transform the complex input coming through our eyes into neural codes that reflect our experience and support our every-day behavior. I use large-scale neuroimaging data and naturalistic designs in combination with modeling approaches that connect brain data to behavior and to modern AI systems of vision and language.

Another focus is on fostering reproducible and community-driven neuroscience by building, sharing, and maintaining openly available datasets that drive scientific discovery.
